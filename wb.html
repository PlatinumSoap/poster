EARTHQUAKE
PREPAREDNESS

DROP   COVER   HOLD ON

Be ready before the ground shakes.


INDOORS

Stay inside. Drop to the ground.
Take cover under a sturdy desk or table.
Hold on until the shaking stops.
Stay away from windows, glass, and heavy furniture.
Do not use elevators. 		OUTDOORS

Move to an open area away from buildings,
power lines, trees, and streetlights.
Drop to the ground and cover your head and neck.
Stay there until the shaking stops.
