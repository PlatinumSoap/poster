Be Aware Before The Ground Shakes

INDOORS

Drop to the floor against an interior
wall and protect your head and neck with
your arms if you are not near a desk or table.

Do not go outside until well
after the shaking stops!

OUTDOORS

Move to a clear area
if you can safely do so.

Avoid buildings, power lines,
and trees.

(Click star for NDMA info)

In bed

Hold on and stay there, protecting
your head with a pillow. You are
less likely to be injured staying
where you are.

Broken glass on the floor
can cause injuries; be
sure to put shoes on
before stepping out!

Driving

When able, safely pull over to the side
of the road, stop, and set parking brake.
Avoid overpasses, power lines, signs,
trees, and falling hazards.

Stay inside the vehicle
until shaking stops. If wires
fall on vehicle, wait for help.

In a public building or theater

If in a theater seat, duck down and
protect your head and neck with arms.
Don't try to rush out until shaking stops.

Then walk out slowly,
watching carefully for
fallen debris or aftershocks.

Near the shore

If you feel a strong quake, hear a
warning, or notice water withdrawing,
evacuate immediately to higher ground.

Tsunami waves may
continue for hours. Do not
return until all-clear is given.
